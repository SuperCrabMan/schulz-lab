01 / WHY WE STUDY IT

Stability is not
the absence of change.

Every nervous system faces a fundamental challenge: neurons must be flexible enough to adapt, yet reliable enough to generate behavior.

The Schulz Lab investigates this balance by asking how neurons regulate excitability, how circuits respond when normal inputs disappear, and how adaptive changes can support the recovery of function. We connect molecular mechanisms to electrical activity and, ultimately, behavior.

03 / HOW WE DO IT

From molecules
to movement.

We move between levels of organization because no single technique can explain a whole circuit.

Explore a collaboration ↗
01

Single-cell molecular profiling

Measuring gene expression in identified neurons to examine molecular diversity and regulation.

02

Electrophysiology

Recording neuronal and circuit activity to link molecular changes with physiological function.

03

Quantitative & computational analysis

Integrating expression, physiology, and behavior to test mechanistic hypotheses.

04

Comparative model systems

Studying accessible circuits, especially in leeches and crustaceans, to reveal general principles.
